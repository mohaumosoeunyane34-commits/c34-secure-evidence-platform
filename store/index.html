Platform

One security workflow.

Designed to connect intelligence, investigation and evidence management into a traceable workflow.

01 / INTELLIGENCE

OSINT

Organize authorized public-source research, domains, technical intelligence, usernames, sources and investigative findings.

02 / INVESTIGATION

Case Management

Structure investigations around cases, subjects, findings, notes and chronological investigative activity.

03 / EVIDENCE

Evidence Integrity

Register evidence, verify integrity and maintain records supporting controlled evidence workflows.

04 / CUSTODY

Chain of Custody

Record authorized evidence transfers between custodians and preserve a traceable custody history.

05 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Audit

Maintain records of important platform actions to support accountability and review.

06 / REPORTING

Investigation Reports

Transform structured case information into professional, source-aware investigation documentation.

Intelligence

From public information to structured findings.

The C34 intelligence layer is designed around source attribution, repeatable collection and clear separation between observed facts and analyst interpretation.

Collection

Domains, DNS information, IP and network intelligence, certificates, technology information, public usernames and other authorized open-source material.

Correlation

Connect related observations, sources, subjects and findings inside a structured investigation.

Source Tracking

Preserve where information came from and when it was collected, supporting reproducibility and review.

Analyst Context

Separate collected information from interpretation, confidence and limitations instead of presenting assumptions as facts.

Evidence

Traceability from discovery to preservation.

C34 connects investigation findings with controlled evidence records and auditable custody events.

CASE ↓ INTELLIGENCE FINDING ↓ SOURCE REFERENCE ↓ EVIDENCE RECORD ↓ INTEGRITY VERIFICATION ↓ AUTHORIZED CUSTODY TRANSFER ↓ AUDIT TRAIL ↓ INVESTIGATION REPORT
C34 is intended for authorized investigations and controlled evidence workflows. Sensitive credentials, private keys and real confidential evidence must never be placed in the public repository.
